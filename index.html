How it works

Four gentle steps to a playroom that works

  1. Free consultation

    A relaxed chat about your space, your family's needs, and what everyday life looks like at home.

  2. Your custom plan

    We assess your space, in person or virtually, and create a layout plan built around how your child plays.

  3. Bring it to life

    Follow the plan at your own pace, or let us source, sort, and set up everything for you.

  4. Keep it growing

    Add seasonal check-ins to refresh your playroom as your child reaches new stages.
