I have a background in early childhood development, and I'm a mom. Both have taught me the same thing: young children love to do things for themselves. When toys are easy to see, easy to reach, and have a simple place to go back to, little ones can choose their play and help tidy up with a lot less help from grown-ups.

I work with families to create playrooms that are calm, practical, and made for the way your child plays, not just pretty for a photo.

My approach

What guides every playroom I set up

Child's-eye view

I plan the room from your child's height. If they can't see it or reach it, they can't use it on their own.

Less, but better

Fewer toys out at once means more focused play. The rest are stored and rotated so old favourites feel new again.

Real life, real families

Your home doesn't need to look like a magazine. I create systems that are simple enough to keep up on a busy weeknight.

Working with me

I'm warm, practical, and never judgmental. Every home with young children gets messy, and that's completely normal. My job is to make the everyday a little easier.
