How It Started

When I became a parent, that passion took on a new purpose. We didn't have much room, so I learned to make our small play space functional for my child. As my little one grew, I kept adjusting it to fit each new stage, from baby toys within easy reach to a setup she could tidy all on her own.

I hold a degree in Early Childhood Development and spent 10 years working in early learning settings, where every space is designed around the children who use it. I learned how much a well-planned space matters: toys children can see, shelves they can reach, and a clear home for everything.

Now I bring that experience into your home. Together, we'll create a playroom that's calm, functional, and easy for your whole family to keep up.

The Functional Playroom Approach

Made for Little Hands

Every playroom is designed around your child, with toys they can see, reach, and choose on their own.

A Home for Everything

Clear bins, simple picture labels, and a spot for every toy make cleanup easy for kids and grown-ups.

Room to Grow
