The Functional Playroom ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, mainly through the contact form or by email. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your town or neighbourhood
  • Your children's ages, if you choose to share them
  • Anything you write in your message

Please don't send sensitive information about your children, such as health or medical details. We don't need it to help with your playroom.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your free consultation
  • To plan and provide the services you book
  • To send invoices and keep basic business records

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information, and we won't add you to a mailing list without asking first.

Photos of your home

We will never share photos of your home or playroom (for example, on this website or social media) without your written permission.
