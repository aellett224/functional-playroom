The Playroom Roadmap

Perfect for DIY parents who need an expert game plan to carry out on their own time.

Investment: $250–$350 (flat fee)
Available: in person in North York and Toronto, or virtually anywhere

What's included

  • A 1-hour deep dive assessment of your current playroom layout and your family's biggest pain points
  • An analysis of your child's specific play habits, attention span, and clean-up struggles

The Signature Space Transformation

Our signature all-inclusive package, taking you from chaotic clutter to a calm, fully functional room.

Investment: $850–$1,500

What's included

  • Everything included in The Playroom Roadmap, plus full hands-on organization
  • Complete decluttering and sorting of toys based on frequency of use and developmental milestones

  • Sourcing, purchasing, and installing all storage solutions, bins, and customized visual labels*

Best for

Busy parents who want a turnkey, stress-free transformation completed from start to finish.

The Playroom Growth and Maintenance Plan

A seasonal partnership to make sure your playroom grows at the speed of your child.

Investment: $200–$300 per session (billed quarterly), or a discounted annual package of $750/year

What's included

  • Quarterly check-ins (4 visits per year) to update the playroom as your child's milestones, interests, and developmental needs shift
  • Re-sorting and swapping out toy rotations to keep the space fresh and engaging
  • Adjusting the physical environment to support new goals (e.g. moving from toddler play to independent homework and craft stations)
  • Routine maintenance and review of visual labels to make sure they still fit your family's daily flow

Best for

Families wanting long-term support to keep clutter from creeping back as their kids grow.

Questions

Common questions

What happens at the free consultation?

We have a relaxed conversation (by phone or video) about your space, your children's ages, and what's not working right now. We'll share some first ideas and explain which service fits best. There's no obligation to book.

What ages do you work with?

Our focus is young children, from babies through the early school years. The setup changes a lot across these ages, and we plan for where your child is now and what's coming next.

Do I have to get rid of lots of toys?

Not at all. Many toys can be stored and rotated rather than donated. You always have the final say on what stays, what's stored, and what goes.

Do I need to buy new storage?

Not always. We start with what you already have. With The Playroom Roadmap, we recommend storage and you choose what to buy. With The Signature Space Transformation, we agree on a product budget together first, and you're billed only for the products used, with receipts.

Should my child be home during setup?

Usually it's easiest to do the setup while your child is out, then let them discover their "new" playroom. We're happy to work around your family's schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We offer in-home services in North York and across Toronto. The Playroom Roadmap is also available virtually, so families anywhere can work with us.

Not sure which option is right?

That's exactly what the free consultation is for. Tell us a little about your space and we'll find the best fit.
