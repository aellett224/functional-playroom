Questions

Common questions

What happens at the free consultation?

We have a relaxed conversation (by phone or video) about your space, your children's ages, and what's not working right now. I'll share some first ideas and explain which service fits best. There's no obligation to book.

What ages do you work with?

My focus is young children, from babies through the early school years. The setup changes a lot across these ages, and I plan for where your child is now and what's coming next.

Do I have to get rid of lots of toys?

Not at all. Many toys can be stored and rotated rather than donated. You always have the final say on what stays, what's stored, and what goes.

Do I need to buy new storage?

Not always. I start with what you already have. If something new would really help, I'll give you a short list with options at different price points.

Should my child be home during setup?

Usually it's easiest to do the setup while your child is out, then let them discover their "new" playroom. I'm happy to work around your family's schedule.

What areas do you serve?

I offer in-home services in [Your City] and nearby areas. The Virtual Playroom Plan is available anywhere.

Not sure which option is right?

That's exactly what the free consultation is for. Tell me a little about your space and we'll find the best fit.
